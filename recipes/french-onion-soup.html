French Onion Soup

Description

French onion soup is a classic French dish known for its rich, savory flavor and comforting warmth. Made with slowly caramelized onions simmered in a flavorful beef broth, this soup combines the natural sweetness of onions with the deep, satisfying taste of herbs and seasonings.

Traditionally served in oven-safe bowls, French onion soup is topped with toasted baguette slices and a generous layer of melted Gruyère cheese. The golden, bubbling cheese and crispy bread create a delicious contrast with the warm, flavorful broth underneath.

Ingredients

  • 4 large yellow onions, thinly sliced
  • 3 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup dry white wine
  • 4 cups beef broth
  • 1 teaspoon fresh thyme
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 1 French baguette, sliced
  • 1 cup grated Gruyère cheese

Steps

  1. Peel and thinly slice the onions into even strips.
  2. Heat the butter and olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
  3. Add the sliced onions and cook for 35–45 minutes, stirring occasionally, until deeply golden and caramelized.
  4. Stir in the minced garlic and flour, cooking for another minute.
  5. Pour in the white wine and stir, scraping any browned bits from the bottom of the pot.
  6. Add the beef broth, thyme, and bay leaf. Season with salt and black pepper.
  7. Reduce the heat and let the soup simmer gently for 20–25 minutes.
  8. Meanwhile, slice the baguette and toast the slices until golden and crispy.
  9. Remove the bay leaf and ladle the soup into oven-safe bowls.
  10. Place toasted baguette slices on top of each bowl and sprinkle generously with Gruyère cheese.
  11. Place the bowls under a preheated broiler for 2–4 minutes, until the cheese is melted, bubbling, and golden brown.
  12. Carefully remove the bowls, allow them to cool slightly, and serve warm.
