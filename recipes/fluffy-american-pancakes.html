Fluffy American Pancakes

Description

Fluffy American pancakes are a classic breakfast favorite, loved for their soft, airy texture and lightly golden exterior. Made with simple pantry ingredients, these pancakes are quick to prepare and perfect for a relaxing weekend breakfast.

Served warm with butter, maple syrup, fresh berries, or sliced bananas, these delicious pancakes can be customized with a variety of toppings. Their light texture and slightly sweet flavor make them enjoyable for both children and adults.

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 3½ teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • 1¼ cups milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 3 tablespoons melted butter
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Butter or oil for cooking
  • Maple syrup, for serving
  • Fresh berries or sliced bananas, optional

Steps

  1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt.
  2. In another bowl, whisk together the milk, egg, melted butter, and vanilla extract.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and gently mix until combined.
  4. Avoid overmixing the batter; a few small lumps are perfectly fine.
  5. Allow the batter to rest for approximately 5 minutes.
  6. Heat a nonstick frying pan or griddle over medium heat.
  7. Lightly grease the surface with butter or cooking oil.
  8. Pour approximately ¼ cup of batter onto the pan for each pancake.
  9. Cook for 2–3 minutes until bubbles form on the surface and the edges begin to set.
  10. Flip the pancake carefully and cook for another 1–2 minutes until golden brown.
  11. Repeat with the remaining batter, adjusting the heat if necessary.
  12. Stack the pancakes on a plate and serve warm with butter, maple syrup, and your favorite toppings.
