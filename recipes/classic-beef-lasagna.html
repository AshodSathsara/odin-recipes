Classic Beef Lasagna

Description

Classic beef lasagna is a hearty Italian dish made with layers of tender pasta, rich meat sauce, creamy cheese, and aromatic herbs. Baked until golden and bubbling, this comforting meal is perfect for family dinners and special occasions.

The combination of savory ground beef, tomato sauce, and melted mozzarella creates a delicious balance of flavors and textures. Each slice reveals beautifully layered pasta and filling, making lasagna a satisfying and timeless favorite.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 500 g ground beef
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 800 g canned crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 400 g ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 egg

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 190°C (375°F).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions, then drain and set aside.
  3. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat.
  4. Add the chopped onion and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
  5. Stir in the garlic and ground beef, cooking until the beef is fully browned.
  6. Drain excess fat, then add crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, oregano, and basil.
  7. Season with salt and black pepper, then simmer the sauce for 20 minutes.
  8. In a separate bowl, mix the ricotta cheese, egg, and half of the Parmesan cheese.
  9. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce across the bottom of a baking dish.
  10. Arrange a layer of lasagna noodles over the sauce.
  11. Spread some ricotta mixture over the noodles, followed by meat sauce and mozzarella.
  12. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used, finishing with mozzarella and remaining Parmesan.
  13. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes, then remove the foil and bake for another 15 minutes.
  14. Allow the lasagna to rest for 10–15 minutes before slicing and serving.
